About RinkRush.

Big Game. Mini Rink.

RinkRush Hockey is built around the fun and competition of mini hockey. From quick games in the basement to full-on tournaments with friends, our goal is to make it easy to gear up, customize your setup, and bring your own style to every game.

We believe mini hockey should be fun, competitive, and open to everyone. Pick your gear, build your lineup, and make every shot count.
